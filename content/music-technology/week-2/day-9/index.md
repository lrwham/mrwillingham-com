{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Fills

A **fill** is a short phrase that adds interest and marks the end of a section — in a 16-step pattern, usually the last four steps. Both ways to make one are on [Fills](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#fills): add sounds (toms and a crash in steps 13–16), or take away and move sounds (stop the hi-hat after 13, move the last snare to 16).

Try both grids, then make your own pattern with a fill. It still has to be built on the rock beat — someone listening should hear the rock beat underneath.
