## Work Session

We're going to add some fills to the Rock Beat pattern.

### Fills

Fills are short musical phrases that add interest to a beat. They are often used to transition between sections of a song. A fill usually lands at the *end* of a phrase — in a sixteen-step pattern, that is normally the last four steps.

#### Add Sounds

One option for a fill is to add some new sounds like toms and crash cymbals.

#### A Bit of Both

Obviously you can combine these ideas. Try making your own drum pattern with a fill.

Your pattern still has to be built on the basic rock beat — someone listening should be able to hear the rock beat underneath your fill.

## Closing

### Share Out

Export one drum pattern with a fill as an MP3 track and turn it in.

#### How to Export an MP3

1. Play your pattern one last time and listen to the whole thing.
2. Open the **File** menu in Soundtrap.
3. Choose **Export**, then choose **MP3**.
4. Save the file somewhere you can find it again.
5. Upload the MP3 file to the assignment. **Do not submit a share link today — this assignment needs the file.**
