## Warmup: What Is MIDI?

We watch a video in which Colin builds a drum machine with microcontrollers and MIDI. Answer in your head: what do the letters **M-I-D-I** stand for? Is MIDI *sound* or *instructions*? What is **velocity**?

{{% checkpoint %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Patterns Beatmaker

Soundtrap's built-in drum machine: each row is a drum, each column a moment in time, click a box and that drum plays. Set it up per [Patterns Beatmaker](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#patterns-beatmaker) — 1/8 notes, 2 measures, 16 steps.

Then program [The Rock Beat](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#the-rock-beat) one drum at a time: hi-hat on every step, kick on 1, 5, 9, 13, snare on 3, 7, 11, 15. Press play after each row. Finished? Mute the hi-hat for one pass and notice what the beat loses. Then try the extras on the reference page — a crash row, 1/16 steps, four measures, velocity mode.
