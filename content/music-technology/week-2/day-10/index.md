## Work Session: Four on the Floor

The kick plays on **every** beat. Today you **play** it in instead of clicking a grid — [Recording a Live Drum Track](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#recording-a-live-drum-track).

<!-- TODO: list the exact keys you assigned, e.g. "Use A, S, D, and F for kick, snare, closed hi-hat, and crash." -->

**Partner protocol.** You and your table-mate share one controller; each of you works in your own project on your own computer. Round 1, kick: one of you on the controller, the other on the assigned keys. Swap. Round 2, snare: the other way around.

Each track is **four measures**:

1. **Kick** — quarter notes on every beat. Count 1 – 2 – 3 – 4.
2. **Snare** — beats 2 and 4.

Listen back. Not perfect is fine; that is what Part 2 is for.

## Work Session: Quantization

Hits played by hand land a little early or late. **Quantization** snaps them to a grid — rounding for musical timing. Read [Quantization](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#quantization), including the grid table, then quantize both tracks to **1/4 Note**. Everything you played today is on the beat; a 1/16 grid would leave the mistakes where they are.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Quantization

- [ ] Kick and snare both quantized to 1/4 Note and playing cleanly.
- [ ] I can explain quantization as rounding.
