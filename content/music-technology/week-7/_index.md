## Unit: Sound for TV, Film & Games

Urban Runner wraps up on Monday with uploads and peer voting. The rest of the week is about making sounds from scratch: setting up the class microphones, recording found sounds, and transforming them with effects. Friday starts the **Sound Design Project** — pick a silent clip and build its whole soundtrack around a sound you recorded yourself.
