## Unit: Sound for Screen

Back from break, Urban Runner wraps up Monday with uploads and peer voting. The rest of the week is about making sounds from scratch: setting up the class microphones, recording found sounds, and transforming them with effects. Friday starts the **Sound Design Project** — pick a silent clip and build its whole soundtrack around a sound you recorded yourself.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Date      | Topic                                  | Summary                                                                                                  |
| --- | --------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 30  | Mon 11/30 | [Upload and Vote](day-30/)             | Export Urban Runner as a video, upload it, watch six assigned classmates, and vote for a top three.       |
| 31  | Tue 12/1  | [Recording Day](day-31/)               | Learn how analog and digital recording work, then set up, test, and tear down the microphones.            |
| 32  | Wed 12/2  | [Take Out the Trash](day-32/)          | Clean up your computer's storage, then set mic gain with headroom and record found sounds.                |

| 34  | Fri 12/4  | [Sound Design Project](day-34/)        | Pick a silent clip and build its soundtrack from your found sound, effects, automation, and the library.  |
