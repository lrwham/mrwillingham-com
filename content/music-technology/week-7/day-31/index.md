## Work Session: Set Up, Test, Tear Down

The movie in one page, plus the clean-take rules: [Recording Setup](/music-technology/reference/recording-setup/). Then do it, in order:

1. **Set up** per the steps — stand, mic, XLR, USB, gain down, a **Voice & Mic** track in a new Soundtrap project named **Found Sounds**, gain up until the meter moves and never hits red.
2. **Test and record** several takes of **paper** (rip slowly, rip fast, crumple) and the **metal stand base** (fingernail, knuckle, pencil), using the take protocol. Listen back; delete bad takes.
3. **Tear down** — save, every gain knob to zero, XLR by the tab, mic off the stand, cable coiled.
