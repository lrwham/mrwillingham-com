{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can build a soundtrack for a silent clip using a sound I recorded myself.
- I can change a sound with effects and shape it with volume automation.
- I can match the key moments on screen to prominent sounds.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Requirements and Rubric

Read the [Sound Design Project](/music-technology/projects/sound-design-project/) page all the way through — the three clips, the five requirements, and the rubric — before you open Soundtrap. Then pick your clip.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] I read all five requirements and the rubric.
- [ ] I picked my clip.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Build the Soundtrack

Set up per the project page: a new video project with your clip, named **Sound Design Project**, your found sound dragged in. Then build against the five requirements — found sound, effects, automation, sound throughout, sync. Effects ideas and the automation curve are linked from the project page.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] My found sound is in the project and audible.
- [ ] At least two effects, at least one volume curve, sound the whole time, every key moment on a sound.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Save. Monday is the last work day; the turn-in is an exported **video** uploaded to CTLS.

Exit ticket: which moment in your clip needs the biggest sound, and what are you going to use for it?

{{% /closing %}}
