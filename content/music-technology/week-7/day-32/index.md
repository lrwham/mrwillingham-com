## Warmup: Take Out the Trash

**Before:** Apple menu → **System Settings → General → Storage**. Hover over the grey band of the Macintosh HD bar and write down the GB available.

1. **Browsers.** Safari: History → **Clear History…** → all history. Chrome and Edge: `cmd + shift + delete` → All time → every box.
2. **Downloads.** Finder → Go → Home → Downloads → `cmd + A`, `cmd + delete`. Then click the **Trash** in the Dock → **Empty**.
3. **After:** check Storage again. How much did you clear?

## Work Session: Gain and Headroom

Set gain for the **loudest** sound you plan to make, then leave it alone — [Recording Setup](/music-technology/reference/recording-setup/#what-makes-a-clean-take). Set up, open **Found Sounds**, make your loudest sound while turning the gain up, stop in the yellow. Record several takes each of ripping paper, tapping a metal water bottle, and a pencil along a spiral binding. Tear down.
