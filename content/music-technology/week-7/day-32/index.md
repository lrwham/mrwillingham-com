## Warmup: Take Out the Trash (15 min)

**Before you start:** Apple menu → **System Settings** → **General** → **Storage**. Hover over the **grey band** of the Macintosh HD bar and write down the **GB available**.

### 1. Browsers

Do every browser on your Mac.

- **Safari:** History menu → **Clear History…** → Clear: **all history** → Clear History.
- **Chrome:** **Cmd + Shift + Delete** → Time range: **All time** → check every box → Delete data.
- **Edge:** **Cmd + Shift + Delete** → Time range: **All time** → check every box → Clear now.

### 2. Downloads

1. Open **Finder**. Go menu → **Home**. Open **Downloads**.
2. **Cmd + A** to select everything, then **Cmd + Delete**.
3. Click the **Trash** in the Dock. Click **Empty** (top right), then confirm.
4. Back to **Storage**. Hover again and write down the new GB available. How much did you clear?

## Work Session: Gain & Headroom

Set gain for the **loudest** sound you plan to make, then leave it alone.

### Key Vocabulary

Gain
: How much the interface boosts the mic.

Headroom
: The space between your loudest peak and red.

1. **Set up**: mic on the stand, XLR to the interface, interface to the computer, gain all the way down. Open **Found Sounds** in Soundtrap.
2. **Set gain**: make your loudest sound while turning the gain up. Stop when the meter peaks in the yellow, never red.
3. **Record**: ripping paper, tapping a metal water bottle, a pencil along the spiral binding of a notebook. Several takes each. One second of silence before and after.
4. **Tear down**: gain to zero, press the tab to unplug the XLR, everything back where it came from.
