### What You Will Do This Year

You will record and edit a podcast, design original sound effects, build beats using MIDI, learn to read music, write an original melody, remix a classical piece into a modern style, and add sound to an animated film.

| Formative | 60%    | Learning checks, warmups, minor projects — taken frequently. |
| Summative | 40%    | Projects and quizzes — taken near the end of each unit. |

## Work Session: Setting Up Soundtrap

Soundtrap is the primary app for this class. It runs in the browser, so your projects follow you to any computer you sit at.

Go to [soundtrap.com](https://www.soundtrap.com) and click **Log in**. Your username is your student email and your password is your lunch number.

**Example**

- Username: `sara.simons@students.cobbk12.org`
- Password: `12345678`

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
If your login does not work, do not keep guessing — raise your hand. Repeated failed attempts can lock your account.
{{< /callout >}}

Once you are in, click **Enter Studio**, start a blank project, and press play so you know the sound is working. Then check your headphone volume before you turn it up.

### Key Vocabulary

DAW (Digital Audio Workstation)
: The software you use to record, edit, and arrange music on a computer. Soundtrap is a DAW.

Project
: One song or recording you are working on inside Soundtrap. Everything you make this year lives inside a project.

Track
: A single layer inside a project — one instrument, one loop, or one recording.

## Closing: Logging Off

Before the bell, follow the log-off procedure. You will do this at the end of every class period:

1. Save your Soundtrap project.
2. Close your browser tabs.
3. Log off the Mac — do **not** just close the lid.
4. Put your headphones back where you found them.

Tomorrow you start your first real project: a scavenger hunt through Soundtrap's loop library.
