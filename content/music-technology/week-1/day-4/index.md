## Objectives

- I can finish all five sets of the Loop Scavenger Hunt.
- I can turn in a worksheet with every loop name and project name filled in.
- I can pick one set to share and explain why.

{{% /objectives %}}

Log in, open Soundtrap, worksheet out. Which sets did you finish yesterday? Which one are you starting with now?

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] I know which set I am starting with.

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Finish, Then Pick One

Project Day 2 of [Loops and Layering](/music-technology/projects/loops-and-layering/#project-day-2-finish-and-pick-one). The requirements have not changed: five separate projects, four loops in each, every name on the worksheet.

Twenty minutes is not enough time to browse. Work in order, stop previewing after three or four options, take the best one.

When all five exist, listen back and **pick one to share tomorrow** — the set where the four loops fit together best, not the one with your favorite single loop. Circle it and be ready to say one sentence: what the four loops are and why they belong together.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] All five projects exist, each with four loops, each named, each saved.
- [ ] Every loop name and project name is on my worksheet.
- [ ] My pick is circled and I can say one sentence about it.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing: Turn It In

Save every project, turn in your worksheet, and log off. Leave your pick easy to find — you play it for the class tomorrow.

{{% /closing %}}
