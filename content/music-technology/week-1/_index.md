## Unit: Loops and Layering

Welcome to Music Technology. Monday is a digital learning day you complete from home: the syllabus, the About Me activity, and a BrainPOP lesson on sound waves. Tuesday through Friday are early-release days, so each lesson is sized to fit a shorter period. You get set up on the classroom Macs and in Soundtrap, then start making music immediately — no instrument required. You dig through Soundtrap's loop library on a scavenger hunt, share your best set with the class, and learn what separates four loops piled on top of each other from an arrangement.

## Week 2 Preview

Next week you finish the loops unit by choosing loops by category and genre, then start making beats: the parts of a drum kit, a beat built from individual loops, what MIDI is, and your own drum pattern in Soundtrap's Patterns Beatmaker. By Friday you play a beat on a MIDI controller and clean up the timing with quantization.
