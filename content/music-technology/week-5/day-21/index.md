## Objectives

- I can compose a complete piece in binary or ternary form with sections of at least eight measures.
- I can write a simple bass line using only the tonic and the dominant.
- I can wrap a form in repeat signs so the whole piece plays twice.
- I can export a score as both an MP3 and a PDF.

## Work Session: Compose One Complete Piece

The summative — Project Day 4 of the [Melody Project](/music-technology/projects/melody-project/#project-day-4-one-piece-one-period). One full piece, start to finish, this period. The seven requirements, the title-block image, and the advice are on that page. The short version: binary or ternary; every section at least 8 measures; letters A–G only, no sharps or flats; mixed rhythms; repeat signs around the whole form; a one-note-at-a-time melody; a **tonic–dominant bass line** under everything; an original title, the subtitle **Binary/Ternary Form Melody Project**, and your name.

An example score with a bass line, and a recording of it, are on today's CTLS post. Eight measures by the halfway point or you are behind.
