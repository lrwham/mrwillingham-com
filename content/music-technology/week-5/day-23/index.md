{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Build It

Follow the three work sessions on [ABA Chords in Soundtrap](/music-technology/projects/aba-chords/): set the key to **Am** and enter the A, B, A progressions one chord per measure; add a simple beat under all 24 measures; then improvise a melody on a lead track using only **A, C, D, E, G**, ending phrases on A.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Key is Am; 24 measures of chords, A B A; A ends on Am, B ends on E.
- [ ] Drums under all 24 measures.
- [ ] A pentatonic melody across all three sections. Project saved.

{{% /checkpoint %}}
