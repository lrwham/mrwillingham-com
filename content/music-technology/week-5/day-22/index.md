## Work Session: Five Minutes to Submit

Not time to write new music — time to export and upload what you have. Title, subtitle, name on the score; MP3 and PDF both in CTLS. When five minutes are up, whatever is uploaded is what gets played.

## Work Session: Listening Party

**Log off. Screens down, headphones off.** Every piece plays on the big speakers, start to finish. Project Day 5 of the [Melody Project](/music-technology/projects/melody-project/#project-day-5-listening-party). Listen for the moment the section changes, the bass line leaning on the dominant and coming home, and one thing the composer did that you did not think of. Every piece gets the same quiet room.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Listening

- [ ] I listened to every piece all the way through.
- [ ] I can name one specific thing another composer did that I want to try.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Your bass line has been two notes. Tomorrow it gets chords.

Closing question: which piece made the form easiest to hear, and what did that composer do to make the section change obvious?
