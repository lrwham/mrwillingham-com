## Unit: Melody & Form

This week everything comes together. Monday you compose one complete piece in binary or ternary form — a melody with a tonic–dominant bass line — and turn it in as an MP3 and a PDF. Tuesday every piece plays out loud at the Listening Party. Wednesday you meet Soundtrap's **Chords** tool and build an ABA chord progression to improvise over. Thursday is review, and Friday is the quiz on music reading and form.
