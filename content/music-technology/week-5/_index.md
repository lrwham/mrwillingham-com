## Unit: Melody and Form

Everything comes together. Monday adds the **dominant** and **cadences** and you write a binary melody that pauses on the dominant and ends on the tonic. Tuesday you compose one complete piece in binary or ternary form — a melody with a tonic–dominant bass line — and turn it in as an MP3 and a PDF. Wednesday every piece plays out loud at the Listening Party. Thursday you meet Soundtrap's **Chords** tool and build an ABA chord progression to improvise over. Friday is review; the quiz is Monday.
