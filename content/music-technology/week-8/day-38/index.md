{{< icon "calendar" >}} **Thursday, December 10th, 2026**

{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can record a podcast intro with proper microphone technique.
- I can trim, split, and delete to clean up a recording.
- I can choose my final topic based on how each intro sounds.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Review Your Intros

Open both intros and reread them out loud, quietly. Each one 4–6 sentences with all five parts; you know how to say every word. Not finished? Finish now — you cannot record without two complete intros.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] Both intros complete and practiced out loud.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Record Both Intros

Project Day 2 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-2-record-your-intros-and-choose-a-topic). One microphone per table, taking turns, **silent** when it is not your turn. Set up per [Recording Setup](/music-technology/reference/recording-setup/), project named **Intro Test**, a track for each intro. Mistake? Do not stop — three seconds of silence, then re-read the line. Listen back to both.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Record

- [ ] Intro A and Intro B recorded and listened to.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Practice Editing

On your intro recordings: **trim** the region edges to cut silence, **split** at the playhead (`cmd + T`) and **delete** a mistake. Cut long pauses and dead air.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Edit

- [ ] Silence trimmed from at least one recording; one mistake cut with split and delete.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing: Choose Your Topic

Decide before you leave. Which intro sounded better? Which topic are you more excited about? Which do you have more to say about? Tear down the mic. Tomorrow you write the full script.

{{% /closing %}}
