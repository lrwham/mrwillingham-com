## Warmup: Score Yourself (5 min)

Open **Sound Design Project** in Soundtrap. Play it once from the first frame to the last. Then give yourself a score on each row of the [rubric](../../week-7/day-34/#rubric): **20**, **10**, or **0**.

| Requirement | Check |
| --- | --- |
| **Found sound** | Can you hear your own recording? |
| **Effects** | Do two or more different effects clearly change your sounds? |
| **Automation** | Does a volume curve match something moving on screen? |
| **Sound throughout** | Is there sound or music from the first frame to the last? |
| **Sync** | Does every key moment land on a prominent sound? |

Anything below 20 is your to-do list for today. Start with the lowest row.

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Finish

Fix your to-do list, lowest row first.

- **No found sound?** Use a take from your **Found Sounds** project, or record one now on your phone.
- **Found sound buried?** Turn it up or turn the other tracks down.
- **Gap of silence?** Stretch your ambience or add a music loop underneath.
- **Late hit?** Zoom in and nudge the sound until it lands on the frame.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
**Stop editing with 10 minutes left.** Exporting and uploading a video takes time.
{{< /callout >}}

## Work Session: Export & Upload

1. Export the project as a **video file** and save it to your computer.

3. Post once. Your post appears after Mr. Willingham approves it.

A Soundtrap link or an MP3 is not a submission. Upload the video.

## Early Finishers: Podcast Brainstorm

Video uploaded? Get a head start on the podcast project.

1. Start a new **Word** document named **Podcast Brainstorm**.
2. Pick **at least four** categories. For each one, write a possible episode title and 1–2 sentences about it.

| Category | Ideas |
| --- | --- |
| **Sports** | A player, a rivalry, a rule you'd change, predictions |
| **Books** | A book you love, a character you relate to, a genre breakdown |
| **Food** | A recipe, a ranking, a food debate |
| **School subjects** | A favorite element, a history event, a math trick |
| **Music** | An artist spotlight, a genre breakdown, how a song was made |
| **Your own interests** | Sneakers, animals, space, cars, fashion, technology |

Choose something you could talk about for three minutes. Keep it school-appropriate. You'll use this list on Wednesday.
