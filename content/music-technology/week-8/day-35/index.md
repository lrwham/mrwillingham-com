## Warmup: Score Yourself

Play **Sound Design Project** once from the first frame to the last, then give yourself 20, 10, or 0 on each row of the [rubric](/music-technology/projects/sound-design-project/#rubric). Anything below 20 is today's to-do list, lowest row first.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] All five rows scored; I know which I am fixing first.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Finish, Then Upload

Work the list with the fixes on [Project Day 2](/music-technology/projects/sound-design-project/#project-day-2-finish-and-submit). **Stop editing with 10 minutes left.** Export as a **video** and upload it to the Sound Design Project discussion on CTLS ([Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/#soundtrap-export-a-video)).

Video uploaded? Get a head start on the podcast: [Project Day 1](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-1-brainstorm) — four categories, a title and a sentence or two each, in a Word document named **Podcast Brainstorm**.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Every row is a 20, or as close as I can get today.
- [ ] My video is exported and uploaded.

{{% /checkpoint %}}
