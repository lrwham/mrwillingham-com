{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can brainstorm podcast topics across several categories.
- I can write a podcast intro that includes a hook, my name, a podcast name, the topic, and a preview.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: What Makes a Good Podcast?

Think of a podcast, channel, or creator you keep coming back to. In Word Online, answer: what is it about, why do you keep listening, and what makes the host good at it? Be ready to share one answer.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] All three answered; one ready to share.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Brainstorm

Project Day 1 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-1-brainstorm). Using the category table on that page, write a possible episode title and a sentence or two for **at least four** categories in a Word document named **Podcast Brainstorm**. Started it on Monday? Finish it. No final decision today.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Brainstorm

- [ ] At least four categories, each with a title and a sentence or two.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Two Intros

Narrow to your two best ideas and write an intro for each — 4–6 sentences, 20–30 seconds read aloud, all five parts: hook, your name, the podcast name, the topic, a preview. The example and the five parts are on the project page. Read each one out loud quietly as you write.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Intros

- [ ] Intro A and Intro B written, each with all five parts.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Tomorrow you record both intros, practice editing, and choose your topic. Sleep on it.

{{% /closing %}}
