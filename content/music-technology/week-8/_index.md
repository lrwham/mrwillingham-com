## Unit: Sound for Screen

Monday is the last work day for the **Sound Design Project** — export it as a video and upload it by the end of class. Tuesday is share day: watch six assigned classmates and vote for a top four.

## Unit: Podcasts

Wednesday starts the last project of the quarter. You pick a topic, write and record two intros, and write your script. The finished podcast is due Friday, December 18.
