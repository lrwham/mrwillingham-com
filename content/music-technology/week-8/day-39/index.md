## Work Session: Write the Script

Project Day 3 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-3-write-your-script): your chosen intro revised; main content with 2–3 points in short lines; a conclusion that thanks the listener and signs off; audio annotations in brackets; 250–500 words. Use Read Aloud to hear it.
