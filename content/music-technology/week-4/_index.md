## Unit: Music Reading

This week you keep reading music you have never seen before. Monday is Mystery Transcription: nine untitled melodies, no answer key, and a form where you submit your guess for each one you finish. You write down what is on the page, play it back in MuseScore to check yourself, and only then figure out what the song is. Answers and prizes come out the next day, and the fastest correct guesses win.
