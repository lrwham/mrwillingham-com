## Unit: Music Reading

A four-day week — Tuesday is a student holiday. Monday and Wednesday finish Mystery Transcription: nine untitled melodies, a timestamped guess form, and a reveal with prizes for the fastest correct answers, then the **C major scale** and your first melody.

## Unit: Melody and Form

Thursday brings the **A minor scale**, the **tonic**, and the routine for turning in every MuseScore assignment. Friday is **form**: one piece built in both **binary (AB)** and **ternary (ABA)** with copy and paste.
