## Unit: Music Reading

This week you stop reading other people's music and start writing your own. Monday and Tuesday finish Mystery Transcription — nine untitled melodies, a timestamped guess form, and a reveal with prizes for the fastest correct answers. From Tuesday on you build the tools a composer actually needs: the **C major scale**, then the **A minor scale**, then **form** — the plan that decides which part of a melody comes back and when. You finish the week with an original melody of your own that you share with the class.
