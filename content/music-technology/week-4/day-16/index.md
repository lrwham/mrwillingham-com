## Work Session: Pick One and Finish It

Project Day 3 of [Mystery Transcription](/music-technology/projects/mystery-transcription/#project-day-3-nine-melodies). Get out the **Mystery Transcription** sheet — nine melodies, no titles. **Pick one at random and complete it.** Do not read all nine looking for the easiest. Point, start, finish. Enter a measure, play it, fix it, move on.

Two rules: write what is on the page, not what you think you hear; and if it does not sound like anything, something is wrong — go find it.

{{< callout type="tip" >}}
Finished early? Start another. Every correct guess counts.
