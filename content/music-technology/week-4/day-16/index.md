## Work Session: Part 1 — Pick One and Finish It

Get out your **Mystery Transcription** sheet. There are **nine melodies** on it and none of them have titles.

**Pick one at random and complete it.** Do not read through all nine looking for the easiest one — that is five minutes you do not have today. Point at one, start it, finish it.

If you already started one on Friday, finish that one.

Enter one measure, press **spacebar**, listen, fix it, move on. Do not enter the whole melody and then check it.

{{< callout type="tip" >}}
**Finished early?** Start another one. You may do as many of the nine as you have time for, and every correct guess counts.
{{< /callout >}}

### Two Rules

- **Write what is on the page, not what you think you hear.** If you decide too early what the tune is, you will start entering it from memory and get it wrong.
- **If it does not sound like anything, something is wrong.** Usually a wrong octave, sometimes a rhythm that is close but not exact. Go find it.
