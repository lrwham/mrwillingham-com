## Work Session: Ambience, the Sound Nobody Notices

Watch the clip with everything muted. It feels broken. Read [Sync Points and Ambience](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/#sync-points-and-ambience) — ambience, diegetic and non-diegetic — then build the bed: one ambience loop under the **entire** clip, turned down until you stop noticing it, plus one more sync point of your choice.

Stretch: swap the ambience so the same clip feels tense instead of fun.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] One ambience loop runs under the whole clip, quieter than my effects.
- [ ] I added one more sync point.

{{% /checkpoint %}}
