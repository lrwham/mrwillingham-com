## Unit: Melody and Form

Monday is the quiz on music reading and form.

## Unit: Sound for Screen

From Tuesday the class becomes the entire soundtrack. Everyone scores the same silent clip — *Urban Runner* — in Soundtrap, starting from nothing. Tuesday lays the **ambience** bed and syncs the first coin. Wednesday adds the vehicles and shapes their **volume automation** so a truck approaches and passes the way a real one does. Thursday is **Foley**: chopping a footstep recording into individual steps and placing each on the frame a foot hits the ground. Friday, the last day before Thanksgiving break, you finish the scene; you upload it and vote when we are back.
