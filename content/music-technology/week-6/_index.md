## Unit: Sound for TV, Film & Games

This week the class becomes the entire soundtrack. Everyone scores the same silent clip — *Urban Runner* — in Soundtrap, starting from nothing. Tuesday lays the **ambience** bed and syncs the first coin. Wednesday adds the vehicles and shapes their **volume automation** so a truck approaches and passes the way a real one does. Thursday is **Foley**: chopping a long footstep recording into individual steps and placing each one on the frame a foot hits the ground. The finished scene, with all six required elements, is due Friday.
