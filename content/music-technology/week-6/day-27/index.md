## Work Session: Automate the Vehicles

We do the truck together. Follow along in your own project.

1. On the truck's track, open **Automation** and choose **Volume**.
2. Add a point before the truck enters, one where it passes, and one after it leaves.
3. Drag the points so the volume stays **low for a long time, then rises fast, then drops fast**.
4. Play it back. If the truck sounds like it is fading in evenly, the curve is wrong.

Then do the bus and the car on your own.

### Why the curve is shaped like that

**Inverse square law.** Sound spreads out in every direction. Double the distance and the same sound has to cover four times the area, so you get one quarter of the energy. An approaching truck stays quiet for most of its trip and gets loud fast at the very end. A straight ramp sounds fake. A curve sounds real.

**Bonus: the Doppler effect.** The pitch drops as a vehicle passes — higher coming toward you, lower going away. That is the "neeeeow" of a race car. Volume automation will not do that for you. If you want the truck to really sell it, find a way to make the pitch drop after it passes.
