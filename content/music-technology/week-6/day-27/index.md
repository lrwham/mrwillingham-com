## Work Session: Automate the Vehicles

We do the truck together, then you do the bus and car. The steps and the reason the shape is a **curve** — the inverse square law, plus the Doppler bonus — are on [The Automation Curve](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/#the-automation-curve). Low for a long time, a fast rise, a fast drop. If it fades in evenly, the curve is wrong.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] The truck's volume follows a curve, not a line.
- [ ] The bus and car are automated too, each loudest as it passes.
