## Warmup: Check the List

Open **Urban Runner – Sound Design** and play it once from the start. Score yourself against the six [Required Elements](/music-technology/projects/urban-runner/#required-elements): four coins, three vehicles with curves, all footsteps and all jumps to the green bus, the ambience bed underneath. Write down what is missing, lowest first.

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Finish

Project Day 4 of [Urban Runner](/music-technology/projects/urban-runner/#project-day-4-finish-the-scene). Work the list.

- **Vehicle with no curve?** Open Automation → Volume and shape it ([The Automation Curve](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/#the-automation-curve)).
- **Footsteps drifting?** Zoom in and nudge each one onto the frame.
- **Jump with one sound?** Add the landing.
- **Silence under the scene?** Stretch the ambience loop.

Done with all six? Put a coin on every coin, or try the tense-ambience stretch from Day 26.
