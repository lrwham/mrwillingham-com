Project share days follow the same procedure every time.

## Before You Vote

Your own project has to be posted. Find your post in the assignment's CTLS discussion. Not there? Export and upload now ([Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/)). It still counts as late, but classmates cannot vote for a project that is not posted.

## Watch Your Six

1. Open the **assignment chart for your class period** on today's CTLS post. Find your name and watch the **six** projects listed beside it.
2. If one of your six is not posted yet, check back later in the period. If fewer are posted than the form needs, tell Mr. Willingham.

**Only the six on your list count.** A vote for anyone else is thrown out.

## Vote

1. Rank your favorites from those six, in order — **1** is your favorite. The lesson says how many to rank (three or four).
2. Submit them on the voting form linked in today's CTLS post. **One form, one time.**
3. Use each number once.

Vote for the **work, not the person**. Judge against the project's rubric or required elements — the same rows you were graded on.

Competition points (**1st = 10, 2nd = 6, 3rd = 3, 4th = 1**) decide what plays on the big screen. They do not affect your grade.

**Done early?** Watch past your six. Submit the form only once.
