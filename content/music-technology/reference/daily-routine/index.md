Your instructions are posted on this site every class day. Follow the same routine each time.

## Read Everything First

Before you start **any** work, read the whole page: objectives, warmup, work session, closing. You are looking for three things — what you are turning in, what you need open, and whether you can start the work session on your own or need to wait for Mr. Willingham.

## The Order of a Lesson

1. **Objectives** — what you should be able to do by the end of class.
2. **Warmup** — a short task you can usually start as soon as you sit down.
3. **Work Session** — the main activity.
4. **Closing** — an exit ticket, a share-out, or a wrap-up.

Each warmup and work session ends with a **checkpoint**. Use it to confirm you did everything before moving on.

## Where Things Live

- **This site** — daily instructions, project guides, and reference pages.
- **CTLS** ([ctlslearn.cobbk12.org](https://ctlslearn.cobbk12.org)) — assignments, uploads, discussion posts, files Mr. Willingham shares, and the lessons on digital learning days.
- **Synergy** (StudentVUE / ParentVUE) — grades and due dates.
- **Soundtrap** and **MuseScore** — where the work gets made. See [Soundtrap Basics](/music-technology/reference/soundtrap-basics/) and [MuseScore Basics](/music-technology/reference/musescore-basics/).

## What to Bring

- Your agenda
- A pencil or pen

Headphones are in the bin. Check the volume **before** you put them on — the AudioBox gets much louder than you expect.

## If You Are Absent

Check CTLS while you are out so you know what you missed, and ask Mr. Willingham about make-up work when you get back. After three days out in a row, make-up work can be requested from the front office.

## Grades and Late Work

| Category | Weight | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formative | 60% | Learning checks, warmups, minor projects |
| Summative | 40% | Projects and quizzes |

Missing work is a zero until it is turned in. Late work is accepted with a one-time 20 point deduction. How to turn things in: [Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/).

## Logging Off

Do this at the end of every period:

1. Save your project (Soundtrap saves automatically, but check; MuseScore needs `cmd + S`).
2. Close your browser tabs.
3. Log off the Mac — do **not** just close the lid.
4. Put your headphones back where you found them.
5. If you used a microphone or MIDI controller, put it away per the lesson's tear-down steps.
