**MuseScore** is notation software. Soundtrap records and arranges sound; MuseScore writes music down. If it asks you to sign in, skip it — you do not need an account.

## New Score

1. **Create New Score**.
2. Title and composer — see [Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/) for naming rules. The lesson tells you if a specific title is required.
3. Instrument: **Piano**, or any single treble instrument. Choose the grand staff when the lesson asks for a bass line.
4. Key signature: usually **C major** or **A minor** — no sharps, no flats.
5. Time signature: **4/4**. Measures: as the lesson says.
6. Click **Done** and save immediately (`cmd + S`).

## Entering Notes

1. Press **N** to turn on note input. The cursor changes; MuseScore is listening.
2. **Choose the duration first** — click the quarter, eighth, half, or dotted value in the toolbar.
3. Type the letter of the note (**C**, **D**, **E**…) or click it onto the staff.
4. Press **N** again to turn note input off.
5. **Space** plays back.

Three things to know:

- **N starts and stops note input.** Nearly every "MuseScore is doing something weird" problem is note input being on when you thought it was off.
- **`cmd + Z` undoes anything.** Use it freely.
- **Duration first, then the note.** Not the other way around.

Enter one measure, play it back, fix it, move on. Finding one wrong note in four is easy; in thirty-two it is not.

## When It Sounds Wrong

| What you hear | What is usually wrong |
| --- | --- |
| A note is too high or too low, rhythm is right | Wrong octave — select the note and press `cmd + up/down arrow` |
| The measure has too many or too few beats | Wrong duration; check for a dot you did or did not mean |
| MuseScore will not let you add a note | The measure is full, or note input is off |
| Everything shifted over | You inserted instead of replaced — undo and try again |
| It does not sound like anything | Usually a wrong octave or a rhythm that is close but not exact |

## Copy, Paste, Vary

Composers do not write a B section from a blank page. They copy A and change it.

1. Add measures: **Add → Measures → Insert Measures at End of Score**.
2. Click the first measure of A, shift-click its last measure, **copy**.
3. Click the first empty measure, **paste**.
4. Change at least two things in the copy: **register** (select and `cmd + up arrow` for an octave), **rhythm**, **direction**, **starting note**, or **motion** (steps versus leaps). Two is enough; change everything and it stops being the same piece.

Paste A again at the end and binary becomes ternary with one paste.

## Other Tools

- **Numbering or lyrics under notes:** click the first note, press `cmd + L`, type, then **space** to move to the next note.
- **Tempo marking:** **Add → Text → Tempo Marking**, or the Tempo palette. Around 120 BPM is a good default.
- **Title after the fact:** **File → Score Properties**, or double-click the title area at the top of the score.
- **Repeat signs:** the Barlines palette — start repeat at the first measure, end repeat at the last.
- **Adding a bass staff:** **Instruments** panel (press **I**) → add Piano or Bass, or start from the grand staff template.

## Exporting

**File → Export → MP3** and **File → Export → PDF**. Both files go to CTLS. Details and why: [Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/#musescore-export-an-mp3-and-a-pdf).
