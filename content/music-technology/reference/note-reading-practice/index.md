Note reading is a speed skill. You either know the note the moment you see it, or you are counting lines while the music keeps going. You do not learn it by understanding it; you learn it by doing it a few hundred times.

## How to Practice

- Start with the clef you are **worse** at. Finish on the mixed exercise — that is the one that looks like the quiz.
- Aim for a **perfect score twice** on each clef, then beat your own time.
- The bass clef is not optional. Your bass lines are written in it.

## The Quiz

The music reading and form quiz covers three things:

- **Note reading** — naming notes in treble and bass clef.
- **Form** — binary (AB) versus ternary (ABA).
- **Tonic and dominant** — tonic is home; dominant pulls back home.
