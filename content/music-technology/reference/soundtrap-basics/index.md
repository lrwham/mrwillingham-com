Soundtrap is the main app for this class. It runs in the browser, so your projects follow you to any computer.

## Logging In

Go to [soundtrap.com](https://www.soundtrap.com) and click **Log in**. Your username is your student email and your password is your lunch number.

- Username: `firstname.lastname@students.cobbk12.org`
- Password: your lunch number

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
If the login does not work, do not keep guessing — raise your hand. Repeated failed attempts can lock the account.
{{< /callout >}}

Once you are in, click **Enter Studio**, start a blank project, and press play so you know sound is working. Check your headphone volume before you turn it up.

## The Sound Library

Open it with the **music-note icon** on the right side of the screen.

{{< tabs >}}
{{< tab name="1. Set tempo and key first" >}}
Set the **key** and **tempo** at the top of the editor *before* adding loops. A loop stretched far from its original tempo sounds warped.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< tab name="2. Filter" >}}
Use the **Instruments**, **Genres**, and **Type** menus to narrow the list, or type a description into the search box.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< tab name="3. Preview" >}}
Click a loop's play button to hear it before you commit. Preview several.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< tab name="4. Add it" >}}
Click **+** to add the loop to a track.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< /tabs >}}

Pitched loops (bass, synth, piano, guitar, strings, vocal) sound best when they share the same **scale type** — Major or Minor. Drums and percussion work with either.

Use only sounds from inside Soundtrap or files Mr. Willingham posts on CTLS. Other sound sites are not approved resources.

## Saving and Naming

Soundtrap saves automatically, but name every project right away — a project Mr. Willingham cannot find is a project that does not get graded. Lesson pages tell you the exact name to use.

## Adding Video

For sound-for-screen projects: **New Project → video option → Video Library**, or drag an `.mp4` from your Downloads into the project. Unzip downloaded packs first; Soundtrap cannot open a `.zip`.

## Exporting

MP3 and video export steps are on [Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/).

## Key Vocabulary

DAW (Digital Audio Workstation)
: The software you use to record, edit, and arrange music on a computer. Soundtrap is a DAW.

Project
: One song or recording inside Soundtrap. Everything you make lives inside a project.

Track
: A single layer inside a project — one instrument, one loop, or one recording.

Loop
: A short section of audio, usually one to four bars, designed to repeat seamlessly.

Tempo (BPM)
: How fast the music moves, measured in beats per minute.

Measure
: One unit of time on the timeline. Most Soundtrap loops are two or four measures long.

Preview
: Listening to a loop before adding it to your project.

Scale Type
: Whether a pitched loop is **Major** or **Minor**.
