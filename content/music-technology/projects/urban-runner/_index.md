The whole class becomes the soundtrack for the same clip, *Urban Runner*, from Soundtrap's Video Library. Each day adds one layer. Concepts and techniques live on [Sound Design](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/).

## Schedule

| Project Day | Plan |
| ----------- | ---- |
| 1 | **Coin and Ambience** — load the clip, sync one coin sound exactly, lay an ambience bed under the whole scene |
| 2 | **Vehicles and Automation** — a sound on the bus, truck, and car, each with a volume curve |
| 3 | **Footsteps and Foley** — chop a footstep recording into single steps and sync them; jumps get two sounds |
| 4 | **Finish the Scene** — all six required elements done and saved |
| 5 | **Upload and Vote** — export as video, upload, watch six classmates, rank a top three |

The project is named **Urban Runner – Sound Design** and the same project is used all five days.

## Required Elements

The finished project must have all six. This is the rubric.

- [ ] **1.** A coin sound on the **first four coins**.
- [ ] **2.** A sound on the **first bus**, with a **volume curve**.
- [ ] **3.** A sound on the **first truck**, with a **volume curve**.
- [ ] **4.** A sound on the **first car**, with a **volume curve**.
- [ ] **5.** **All footsteps** up until the **green bus**.
- [ ] **6.** **All jumps** up until the **green bus**.

The ambience bed from Day 1 runs under the whole clip. A vehicle with a sound but no curve is not finished.

## Project Day 1: Coin and Ambience

1. Soundtrap → **New Project** → the video option → **Video Library** → **Urban Runner**.
2. Watch it once. Find the moment the runner touches the first coin.
3. Search the library for a coin sound (*coin, chime, bell, ding, sparkle, glockenspiel, triangle*). Drag it to a track and slide it until it lands **exactly** on the coin. If you have to wonder whether it lined up, it didn't.
4. Save as **Urban Runner – Sound Design**.

Then watch with everything muted. Real silence sounds broken. Add one **ambience** loop under the **entire** clip — city, traffic, crowd — and turn it down until you stop noticing it. Add one more sync point of your choice.

Stretch: swap the ambience so the same clip feels tense instead of fun, without changing the picture.

## Project Day 2: Vehicles and Automation

Finish the ambience and put a coin on the first four coins. Then find a car, truck, or engine sound and place one where the **bus** appears, one for the **truck**, one for the **car** — each vehicle on its own track.

Automate the truck together, then the bus and car alone: [the automation curve](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/#the-automation-curve). Low for a long time, a fast rise, a fast drop. If it fades in evenly, the curve is wrong.

## Project Day 3: Footsteps and Foley

Download the **Footstep Sound Pack** from CTLS. Ten files, each a continuous walk or run:

| File | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| `Concrete_Shoes_Walking` | Pavement, walking |
| `Concrete_Shoes_Running` | Pavement, running |
| `Gravel_Ice_Shoes_Walking` | Gravel |
| `NormalWood_Shoes_Walking` | Wood floor |
| `CreakyWood_Boots_Walking` | Old creaky wood |
| `Sand_Boots_Walking` | Sand |
| `Puddle_Walking` | Water |
| `SoftCarpet_Barefeet_Walking` | Carpet, barefoot |
| `Glass_Walking` | Broken glass |
| `Leaves_Rustling` | Leaves |

Pick the surface that matches the runner, drag it into the project, and chop it — [Foley](/music-technology/reference/sound-design/#foley). The files are **not** ready to use: the whole file will not line up and cannot line up. Split out single steps, place each on the frame a foot lands, alternate three or four different steps, and give every jump a takeoff and a landing.

## Project Day 4: Finish the Scene

Check the six required elements. Fix what is missing, lowest first. Save. Anything undone is homework before the upload day.

## Project Day 5: Upload and Vote

Ten-minute final check against the six elements, then stop editing. Export as a **video** and upload it to the CTLS discussion ([Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/#soundtrap-export-a-video)). Then [Peer Voting](/music-technology/reference/peer-voting/): watch your six, rank a top three. The top three play on the big screen.

## Checkpoints

**Day 1**

- [ ] One coin sound lands exactly on the first coin.
- [ ] One ambience loop runs under the whole clip, quieter than the effects.
- [ ] Saved as Urban Runner – Sound Design.

**Day 2**

- [ ] First four coins have a sound.
- [ ] Bus, truck, and car each have a sound on their own track.
- [ ] Each vehicle's volume follows a curve and peaks as it passes.

**Day 3**

- [ ] I split the recording into individual steps and use more than one.
- [ ] Every footstep lands on the frame the foot hits the ground.
- [ ] Each jump has a takeoff and a landing.

**Day 4**

- [ ] All six required elements are done.

**Day 5**

- [ ] My video is uploaded.
- [ ] I watched my six and submitted the voting form once.

## Teacher Notes

- Exit tickets that worked: *Name one sound you added that has no visible source on screen — what is it doing for the viewer?* (Day 1); *Why is the truck's volume curve not a straight line?* (Day 2); *Why can't you drag the whole footstep recording onto the timeline?* (Day 3).
- Day 3 is the heaviest day. Students who finish footsteps early should go back and put a coin on every coin, not just the first four.
- A break between Day 4 and Day 5 is fine; the project is saved in Soundtrap.
- Post the footstep pack, the assignment chart, and the voting form on CTLS.
