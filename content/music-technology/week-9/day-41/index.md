## Objectives

- I can give constructive feedback on a classmate's script.
- I can revise my script based on feedback.
- I can finalize my script so it is ready to record.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Check Your Own Script

Reread it against the checklist: intro with all five parts, 2–3 main points, a conclusion, audio annotations, 250–500 words. Fix anything missing before you share.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] My script has every required section.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Peer Feedback

Project Day 5 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-5-peer-feedback-and-final-script). Share your script with **Can Review** permission and paste the link in the CTLS discussion. Read **two** classmates' scripts and leave specific feedback — what works and why, what is unclear and how to fix it. "It's good" is not feedback.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Feedback

- [ ] My script link is in the discussion.
- [ ] Two scripts read, specific feedback left on each.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Revise and Turn In

Read the feedback you got. Fix what is unclear, awkward, or missing. Read Aloud once more; fix spelling with the Editor; break long paragraphs into short lines. Turn in the final script on CTLS. It is due today and must be ready to record tomorrow.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Final Script

- [ ] Revised from feedback and checked with Read Aloud.
- [ ] Final script turned in on CTLS.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Tomorrow is recording day. Bring your final script and know where your intro music MP3 is.

{{% /closing %}}
