## Work Session: Record Your Episode

Project Day 6 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-6-record). Set up per [Recording Setup](/music-technology/reference/recording-setup/). New Soundtrap project named **Podcast**: intro music on its own track, a **Voice & Mic** track for you. One mic per table, taking turns, silent otherwise.

Record **one section at a time**. Mistake? Three seconds of silence, re-read the line. Listen back; re-record anything unclear, quiet, or rushed. Tear down when you are done.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Mic set up correctly; intro music imported.
- [ ] Full script recorded, listened back, weak sections re-recorded.
- [ ] Gain to zero, mic and cable put away.
