## Unit: Podcasts

The last week of the quarter. Monday you build intro music from loops. Tuesday you trade scripts for peer feedback and turn in your final script. Wednesday you record, Thursday you edit, and Friday you export your finished podcast, upload it to CTLS, and listen to your classmates' episodes.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Date      | Topic                                  | Summary                                                                                              |
| --- | --------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 40  | Mon 10/5  | Day 40: Intro Music                    | Layer 2–4 loops into 10–20 seconds of intro music that builds.                                       |
| 41  | Tue 10/6  | Day 41: Peer Feedback & Final Script   | Read two classmates' scripts, give specific feedback, then revise and turn in your final script.     |
| 42  | Wed 10/7  | Day 42: Record Your Podcast            | Set up the microphone and record your script one section at a time.                                  |
| 43  | Thu 10/8  | Day 43: Edit Your Podcast              | Re-record weak sections, cut mistakes, reduce noise, balance levels, and add music and sound effects. |
| 44  | Fri 10/9  | Day 44: Finish & Share                 | Export an MP3, upload it to CTLS, and listen to and comment on classmates' podcasts.                 |
