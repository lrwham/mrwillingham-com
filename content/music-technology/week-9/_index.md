## Unit: Podcasts

The last week of the quarter. Monday you build intro music from loops. Tuesday you trade scripts for peer feedback and turn in your final script. Wednesday you record. Thursday and Friday are early-release days: Thursday you edit, and Friday you export your finished podcast, upload it to CTLS, and listen to your classmates' episodes.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Date      | Topic                                              | Summary                                                                                              |
| --- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 40  | Mon 12/14 | [Intro Music](day-40/)                             | Layer 2–4 loops into 10–20 seconds of intro music that builds, and export it.                        |
| 41  | Tue 12/15 | [Peer Feedback and Final Script](day-41/)          | Read two classmates' scripts, give specific feedback, then revise and turn in your final script.     |
| 42  | Wed 12/16 | [Record Your Podcast](day-42/)                     | Set up the microphone and record your script one section at a time.                                  |
| 43  | Thu 12/17 | [Edit Your Podcast](day-43/)                       | Early release. Re-record weak sections, cut mistakes, reduce noise, balance levels, add music.       |
| 44  | Fri 12/18 | [Finish and Share](day-44/)                        | Early release. Export an MP3, upload it to CTLS, and listen to and comment on classmates' podcasts.   |
