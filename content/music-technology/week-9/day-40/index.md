## Work Session: Build the Intro Music

Project Day 4 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-4-intro-music). New Soundtrap project named **Intro Music**. Genre first, then 2–4 loops in the same key and tempo — a beat, a bass, a melody — staggered so it builds ([Arranging Loops](/music-technology/reference/arranging-loops/)). **10–20 seconds.** It plays under your first line and fades.

Export it as an MP3 ([Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/#soundtrap-export-an-mp3)) and keep the file where you can find it on recording day. Finished? Keep working on your script — it is due tomorrow.
