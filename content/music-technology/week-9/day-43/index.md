{{< icon "calendar" >}} **Thursday, December 17th, 2026** — *Early Release*

{{% alert "Short Class" %}}
Early release today and tomorrow. Microphones are available for about five minutes per person, taking turns; the rest is editing.
{{% /alert %}}

{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can edit a recorded episode by removing mistakes, adjusting levels, and adding music and effects.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Listen Back

Open **Podcast** and listen to the whole recording without editing. Note what truly needs re-recording — major mistakes only. Small ones get cut.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] Full listen-through; re-record list written down.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Re-record, Then Edit

Project Day 7 of the [Podcast](/music-technology/projects/podcast/#project-day-7-edit). Take your five minutes with the mic when it is your turn. Then the editing checklist: trim dead air, split and delete mistakes at the three-second silences, noise reduction, voice at a consistent level with music and effects quieter, intro music faded under your first line, transitions, sound effects on their own track, outro music.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Mistakes and long pauses cut.
- [ ] Voice, music, and effects balanced.
- [ ] Intro and outro music placed.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Save. Tomorrow: one last listen, export, upload, and listen to classmates.
