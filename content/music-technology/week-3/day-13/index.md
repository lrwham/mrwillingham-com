## Work Session: Rhythm

Yesterday was **which** note. Today is **how long**. The note values table, rests, dots, the time signature, and counting are on [Music Reading 101](/music-technology/reference/music-reading-101/#note-values). Say the counts out loud with the class: 1 – 2 – 3 – 4, then 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.

This also explains quantizing. A hi-hat playing eighth notes needs a 1/8 grid; quantize it to 1/4 and half of it vanishes. It was never magic. It was always just note values.

## Work Session: Open MuseScore

MuseScore writes music down; Soundtrap records and arranges sound. For the rest of this unit you use both. Follow **New Score** and **Entering Notes** on [MuseScore Basics](/music-technology/reference/musescore-basics/):

1. New score titled **LastName_First Steps**: treble clef, C major, 4/4, 4 measures.
2. **N** for note input, choose the quarter note, enter four C's — one full measure. **N** again to stop. Space to play.

Three things to know: **N** starts and stops note input; `cmd + Z` undoes anything; choose the duration first, then the note.
