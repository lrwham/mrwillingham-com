## Objectives

- I can build a short song from at least five loops and export it as an MP3.
- I can record a beat two ways — MIDI controller and the keys — and quantize both.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Loop Song

Graded. **Twenty minutes total, including the export.** The requirements are [Project Day 5](/music-technology/projects/loops-and-layering/#project-day-5-loop-song) of Loops and Layering: new project named **LastName_LoopSong**, at least five different loops, more than one instrument family, 8–16 measures, loops on measure lines, something changes. Export the MP3 and upload the file to CTLS ([Turning In Work](/music-technology/reference/turning-in-work/#soundtrap-export-an-mp3)). Leave the last few minutes for the export.

A few loop songs play for the class. For each, be ready to name one loop choice that worked and one thing you would change. Still exporting? Finish during this block — the listening round is the deadline.

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Two Beats

Review [Quantization](/music-technology/reference/beat-making/#quantization): kick and snare on the beat take a 1/4 grid; an eighth-note hi-hat takes a 1/8 grid, or you erase half of it.

Then two short beats, four measures each, both quantized. Same partner protocol as Friday: six minutes on the **MIDI controller** for one of you while the other uses the **assigned keys**, swap, six more minutes. Keep it simple — kick and snare is plenty. The point is comparing the two ways of playing it in.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Two Beats

- [ ] One beat recorded on the controller, one on the keys, four measures each.
- [ ] Both quantized.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

## Closing

Save and name both projects. Controller unplugged and back on the cart, cable coiled, headphones in the bin.

Closing question: which input method was easier to play in time, and did quantizing fix it either way? Tomorrow we start reading music.
