## Unit: Beat Making

Monday wraps up beat making: a graded loop song, then two short beats — one on the MIDI controller, one on the keys — both quantized.

## Unit: Music Reading

From Tuesday you switch from making sound to reading and writing it. You learn the staff, the treble and bass clefs, and how to find any note on a piano keyboard, then note values, rests, and time signatures. From Wednesday you work in **MuseScore**, where you transcribe Beethoven's Ode to Joy and then a mystery melody you have to identify by ear after you write it down.
