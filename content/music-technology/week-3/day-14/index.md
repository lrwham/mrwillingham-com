## Work Session: Read It, Then Enter It

**Read it before you touch the computer.** Point at each note and say its name, then the rhythm. Which measures repeat? Where are the only notes that are not quarter notes? The melody table on the project page is the safety net if you get lost.

Then enter it: duration first, one measure at a time, play back after every measure. The [When It Sounds Wrong](/music-technology/reference/musescore-basics/#when-it-sounds-wrong) table covers the usual problems. Add a tempo marking around 120 BPM.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] I said every note name out loud before entering anything.
- [ ] All 8 measures entered, each with exactly four beats, checked by ear as I went.
- [ ] It sounds like Ode to Joy.

{{% /checkpoint %}}
