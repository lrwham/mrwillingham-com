## Unit: Introduction to Design

This week the skimmer stops being a craft project and becomes a dataset. After Monday's digital learning day you finish the build and learn the launch protocol on Tuesday, then launch on Wednesday in a tight 30-minute period — four lanes, three trials each, every distance measured in feet. Thursday is a graded BrainPOP statistics day that teaches the vocabulary: mean, median, mode, range, and what center and spread mean. On Friday **Activity 1.4: Skimmer Statistics** begins for real: you type the whole class's launch data into Excel, calculate ten statistics by formula, and draw a box and whiskers plot by hand. Everyone built from the same dimensioned sketch, so the spread in the data is the spread in how accurately people measured, cut, and folded.
