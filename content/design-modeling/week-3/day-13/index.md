{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can test a product using a consistent, repeatable procedure.
- I can measure and record distance data accurately in feet, to the nearest half foot.
- I can recognize that repeated trials of the same design produce different results.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Thirty Seconds at the Lane

At your lane, the protocol gets restated once:

1. **Feet, always** — nearest half foot, one decimal. Only .0 and .5 endings exist.
2. **Measure to the farthest end of the skimmer** — the very tip, even if it lands sideways.
3. **Three trials, no do-overs.** A bad launch is data.
4. **Write your own numbers** in your trial table.

Then roles for the first launch: **Lane Captain** records, two **Judges** agree on the distance and call CLEAR, **Alternate** steps in wherever needed. Roles rotate one seat each launch.

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Three Rounds

We rotate **by round, not by student** — every student in the lane launches Trial 1 before anyone launches Trial 2. If time runs short, everyone has two trials instead of some having three and some having none.

- **Round 1** — everyone's Trial 1. Launch, Judges call the distance to the tip, Lane Captain records, owner writes it down. About a minute per launch.
- **Round 2** — everyone's Trial 2. Notice: your second number is not your first number. Say out loud what you think changed.
- **Round 3** — everyone's Trial 3. Lanes that finish early re-check that all three numbers on the lane sheet are legible.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Broken skimmer? Tape it, launch it, record it. A repaired skimmer's short flight is legitimate data. So is a launch that goes two feet.
{{< /callout >}}

## Closing

Skimmers in the bin, tapes coiled, lane sheets to Mr. Willingham. Notebooks get checked at the door for one thing: **units.**

Nobody got the same number three times, and nobody in the room matched anybody else. Tomorrow you learn the vocabulary — mean, median, mode, range — that turns all of those numbers into one picture of the whole class.
