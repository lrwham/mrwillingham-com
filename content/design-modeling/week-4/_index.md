## Unit: Introduction to Design

The statistics arc closes and the measurement-and-dimensioning arc opens. Monday's short period walks the whole class through drawing the box plot from the completed class spreadsheet, and Tuesday finishes it with a video warmup before **Activity 1.5: Dialed In** begins — you meet the dial caliper and make thumbnail sketches with labeled dimensions. Wednesday goes all in on 1.5: six labeled caliper measurements of a 20-cube object, then dimensioning conventions and a guided dimensioning of a six-cube multiview sketch. Thursday and Friday are sketching practice — choosing the best front view, drawing aligned TOP, FRONT, and RIGHT views, and the quiz's missing-front-view format. The Unit 1 Quiz has moved to **Friday, September 4** so there is real time to practice.
