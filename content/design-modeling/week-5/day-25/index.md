## Objectives

- I can show four weeks of work on the Unit 1 Quiz — on paper, on my own.
- I can finish my Tinkercad name tag quietly when my quiz is turned in.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Before the Quiz

- On your desk: **ruler, pencil, calculator.** Nothing else. Loaner rulers and calculators are at the front if you forgot — get one before we start.
- Notebooks and study guides in your backpack, backpack on the floor.
- Ten minutes of quiet study time before papers go out — use it on whatever you named in yesterday's closing sentence.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] Ruler, pencil, calculator on the desk — nothing else.
- [ ] Notebook and study guide put away.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: The Quiz

- Read each question completely before answering — more than one has a trap for skimmers, and you have seen every trap before.
- **Show your work** on the statistics problems: the ordered list and the arithmetic earn points even if a final number slips.
- On the drawing item, remember the order: get the view right first, **then** dimension. You cannot earn dimension points on a wrong shape.
- Done early? Check the drawing item against the six errors, then flip your quiz over and raise your hand.

### After the quiz

When your paper is turned in: laptop open, Tinkercad, and finish your name tag from yesterday — or, if it is done, free tinker: build anything you like using the tools from this week. Headphones-quiet until every quiz is in.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Quiz turned in with my name, period, and date on it.
- [ ] Name tag finished, or free tinker in progress, quietly.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing

Perspective: four weeks ago most of you had never read a dial caliper, drawn an aligned three-view sketch, or found a quartile by hand. Today you do all three on one quiz. Whatever today's score, that distance is real — and Monday we get back to building.

Before you leave: loaner ruler or calculator returned to the front, logged out of Tinkercad.

Next week: **Activity 2.2 — For Good Measure.** Hollow containers, surface area, and volume in Tinkercad — the road to the Puzzle Cube Design Challenge.
