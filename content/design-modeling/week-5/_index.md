## Unit: Introduction to Design

Monday runs the quiz's biggest item start to finish on one small object: sketch it in isometric plus three aligned views, then add a complete set of dimensions — everything a builder needs and nothing extra. The Unit 1 Quiz is **Friday, September 4**: paper only, bring a ruler, a pencil, and a calculator. The study guide goes home Tuesday, with one short section to read each night.

## Unit: Modeling

Tuesday **Lesson 2** begins with **Activity 2.1: Taking Modeling to Another Dimension**. Your sketching skills go digital: you join our Tinkercad class, work through the Tinker Time tutorials, build a solid model straight from a multiview sketch, resize objects two ways, cut holes, and design a name tag that has to hit exact dimensions. Lesson 2 ends in the Puzzle Cube Design Challenge.
