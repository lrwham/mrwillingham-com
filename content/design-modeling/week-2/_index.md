## Unit: Introduction to Design

This week the class pivots from hands-on building to technical communication. In **Activity 1.2: A Picture Is Worth a Thousand Words** you learn the four types of sketches and work up the ladder — a thumbnail sketch of a classroom object, isometric sketches using the box-it-then-cut-it method, and a multiview sketch with TOP, FRONT, and RIGHT views lined up the way a builder needs them. Then **Activity 1.3: Measuring Matters** switches to measurement: reading customary and metric rulers to 1/16 inch and 0.1 cm, and reading a dimensioned sketch. On Friday you build a paper skimmer from that sketch. The skimmer only flies if the sketching and the measuring were both accurate.
