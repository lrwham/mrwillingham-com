## Warmup: Plan Your Pitch

Head a new page: 9/30/2026 · **"Pitch + Tinkercad Practice"**. Sit with your team, get out your Data Sheet, and write one line for each of these:

1. **Our cube** — how many pieces, and what makes it tricky.
2. **Our data** — the median time, the fastest and slowest times, and how many DNFs.
3. **Our rating** — easy, average, or difficult, using the class difficulty times in your packet. Your median decides it, not your opinion.
4. **One change** you would make to the design, and why.

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Pitches

Sixty seconds per team, in team order. Bring the wooden cube and the Data Sheet to the front. Hit the four lines from the warmup: the cube, the data, the rating, and one change. Numbers beat adjectives. "Our median was 84 seconds" says more than "it was pretty hard."

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Tinkercad Practice

Tomorrow's quiz includes one Tinkercad question: build a solid model from a multiview drawing. Today you practice one like it. **The drawing is in today's CTLS post.** Sign in to [Tinkercad](https://www.tinkercad.com) with the link for your class period in the CTLS post. All dimensions are in millimeters.

1. **Build the main box first.** Type the three overall dimensions into the size boxes. Do not drag.
2. **Add the cylinder hole.** Get its diameter from the drawing, its depth from the view that shows it as hidden lines, and its location from the dimensions to the edges. A hole that is only partway down has to be raised off the Workplane.
3. **Add the rectangular hole.** Same idea: size first, then location. Check it from the FRONT view and the RIGHT view before you move on.
4. **Group** everything so the holes cut. Then check every view against the drawing, one dimension at a time.
5. **Rename** the design `Practice – First Last`.
6. **Export** → **.STL**. Then **Collaborate** (the person-with-a-plus icon) → **Generate new link** → **Copy link**.
7. **Turn it in on CTLS**: attach the STL file and paste the share link in the text box. Paste is Ctrl V on Windows, Command V on Mac.

{{< callout type="important" >}}
This is exactly how Question 5 gets turned in tomorrow. A file with no link, or a link with no file, is not finished.
{{< /callout >}}

**Done early?** Sketch the TOP, FRONT, and RIGHT views of one of your own puzzle cube pieces in your notebook.
