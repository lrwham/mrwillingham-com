{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Testing With Classmates

Use the same [Puzzle Cube Data Sheet](/design-modeling/week-8/day-35/DM_PuzzleCube_TestingDataSheet.pdf) you started Monday. Lost it? Ask for a new one and copy Trials 1 and 2 from a teammate.

1. **Set up your station** — your wooden cube taken apart with the pieces on the desk, your Data Sheets, a timer.
2. **One teammate stays at the station as the timer.** Everyone else leaves to test other teams' cubes. When Mr. Willingham calls the switch, a different teammate takes over as timer.
3. **Testers:** one try per cube. Do not watch a cube get solved before your turn — it changes the data. No hints from anyone.
4. **Timer:** say "Go," start the clock, and stop it when the 3×3×3 cube is closed. Record the tester's **name**, their **team number**, and the **time in whole seconds**, starting at Trial 3. Take the cube apart before the next tester sits down.
5. **Goal: at least 9 times total**, counting Monday's two. More is better.

{{< callout type="important" >}}
**Stop the clock at 3:00.** A cube not solved in 180 seconds is a DNF (Did Not Finish). Write **180** and check the DNF box. Do not let it keep running.
{{< /callout >}}

{{% /worksession %}}

## Work Session: Your Cube's Data

Back at your station for the last 15 minutes.

1. **Copy every trial** onto every teammate's sheet. Everyone finishes with the same data.
2. **Step 1 — put the times in order**, smallest to largest, in the boxes on the sheet. A DNF counts as 180 and goes at the end.
3. **Step 2 — the five-number summary.** Same method as Monday's warmup:
   - Minimum and maximum are the first and last numbers.
   - The median is the middle number. With an **even** count, add the two middle numbers and divide by 2.
   - Q1 is the median of the lower half and Q3 is the median of the upper half. With an odd count, leave the median out of both halves. With an even count, split the list exactly in half.
   - Fill in the count (n) and the number of DNFs.
4. **Step 3 — the box plot.** Mark the five numbers above the number line on the sheet, box from Q1 to Q3, a line at the median, whiskers to the minimum and maximum. Plot a DNF at 180.
5. **Tape the Data Sheet into your Engineering Notebook.**

**Done early?** Go back to your warmup prediction. Under it, write whether you were right, and use your median as the evidence.
