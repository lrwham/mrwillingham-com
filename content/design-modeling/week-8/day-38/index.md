## The Unit 2 Quiz

The quiz is on paper. Bring a pencil and a calculator. The last question is built in Tinkercad. Keep your laptop closed until you reach it. Sign in to Tinkercad with the link for your class period in the CTLS post.

## Turning In the Tinkercad Question

1. **Group** everything into one solid so the holes cut.
2. **Rename** the design `Unit 2 Quiz – First Last`.
3. **Export** → **.STL**. The file downloads.
4. **Collaborate** (the person-with-a-plus icon) → **Generate new link** → **Copy link**.
5. On CTLS, open the **Unit 2 Quiz – Tinkercad** assignment. **Attach** the STL file. **Paste** the share link in the text box (Ctrl V on Windows, Command V on Mac).

{{< callout type="important" >}}
Both pieces are required. A file with no link, or a link with no file, is not finished. Turn in the paper quiz too.
{{< /callout >}}
