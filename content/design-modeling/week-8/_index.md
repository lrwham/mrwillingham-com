## Unit: Modeling

Welcome back from Fall Break. **Project 2.4: Puzzle Cube Design Challenge** moves into testing. Every team tests the **wooden** cube it glued before break. 3D-printed cubes go only to teams whose design was submitted correctly, and they will not be ready in time for testing.

Monday you practice the five-number summary and draw a box plot by hand, then two teammates each take a timed try at your cube. Tuesday you test with classmates from other teams, then find the five-number summary of your own cube's times and draw its box plot. Everything goes in the Engineering Notebook, and the Data Sheet gets taped in when it is finished.

**Testing rule:** three minutes per try. A cube not solved in 180 seconds is a **DNF** (Did Not Finish).

Wednesday every team gives a 60-second pitch of its cube and its data, and then everyone practices building a solid model from a multiview drawing in Tinkercad. Thursday is the **Unit 2 Quiz**: a paper quiz, plus one Tinkercad model turned in on CTLS as an STL file and a share link.

Friday the final project begins: **Problem 3.1: Therapeutic Toy Design**. It runs six class days and is the final summative for the course. There is no Lesson 3 test.
