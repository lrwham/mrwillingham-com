## Unit: Modeling

A four-day week after Labor Day. Tuesday closes out **Activity 2.1**: the name tag has to meet every design requirement exactly as listed in myPLTW, and it is turned in on CTLS two ways — the STL file attached and the Tinkercad share link pasted in the text box. Anyone who still needs time on the Unit 1 Quiz gets it Tuesday. A name tag not turned in by the bell is homework, due **Friday, September 11**.

Wednesday **Activity 2.2: For Good Measure** begins, and everything in it is in millimeters. You build a hollow box with the subtractive method, then a box container to exact inside and outside dimensions, and then do the math on your own container: how much it holds, how much paint covers it, and what it takes to make ten thousand. Every warm-up this week is measurement — the dial caliper practice tool, then unit conversions the container problems depend on.

Next week: **Activity 2.3: It's For the Birds** — a nest box, in inches.
