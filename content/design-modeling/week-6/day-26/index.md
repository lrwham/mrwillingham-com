## Warmup: Caliper Practice

Head the page (9/8/2026 · **"Name Tag — Link + STL"**), then open the [dial caliper practice tool](/caliper/).

A dial caliper reading is two parts added together: the **beam** gives tenths of an inch, the **dial** gives thousandths. Three decimal places, every time.

1. Read the caliper, type your answer, and press **Check**.
2. Write down at least **five** readings in your notebook, and note how many you got right on the first try.

## Work Session: Finish and Turn In the Name Tag

[Clever](https://clever.com) → **myPLTW** → Design and Modeling → Lesson 2 → **Activity 2.1** → **This One Has Your Name On It**. Sign in to [Tinkercad](https://www.tinkercad.com) with the link for your class period in the CTLS post.

1. **Check every requirement.** Open the design requirements on the myPLTW page and check your tag against each one — base, letters, hole. The numbers on that page are the spec. Match them **exactly as listed**. Type dimensions; do not drag.
2. **Export the STL.** Select every part of the tag → **Group** so it is one solid → **Export** → **.STL**. The file downloads. Rename it `Lastname_Firstinitial_NameTag.stl`. If you do not see it, check your Downloads folder before you ask.
3. **Copy the share link.** With the design open: **Share** → anyone with the link can view → copy. No Share button? Copy the address bar with your design open in the editor.
4. **Turn in on CTLS — two things on one assignment.** **Attach** the STL file. **Paste** the share link in the text box.

{{< callout type="important" >}}
An STL is the handoff file between CAD and a 3D printer. An ungrouped tag exports as loose pieces — group first, and check it from two views before you export. And the assignment is not finished until both pieces are there: a file with no link, or a link with no file, is incomplete.
{{< /callout >}}
