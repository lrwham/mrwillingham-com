Reusable, date-free project guides for the Design and Modeling course. Projects are added here as they are taught and generalized, written as "Day 1 of the project" through "Day N" so they can be dropped into any week.

Nothing has been promoted to the library yet. The first candidates are the Foot Orthosis Instant Design Challenge and the Paper Skimmer build-test-analyze sequence from Unit 1.
