## Warmup: The Syllabus

Find your seat and get comfortable. We walk through the syllabus together — it is printed for you and posted in CTLS.

The things to know:

- **Grades** are 40% summative (projects and quizzes at the end of a unit) and 60% formative (warmups, notebook entries, learning checks, minor projects — taken often).
- **Materials:** a composition notebook (wide-ruled, college-ruled, or quad — your choice) that becomes your **Engineering Notebook**, plus a pencil, pen, and colored pencils. Your laptop and headphones come every day.
- **Late work** gets a one-time 20% deduction. Missing work is a zero until it comes in.
- **Absent?** Check CTLS for what you missed. It is your job to come find the make-up work.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Do not have a notebook yet? Get one this week. Everything you do in this class — every sketch, every measurement, every test result — goes in it, and it is how you study for the quiz.

## Work Session: Procedures and About Me

### How the lab runs

Four things you will do all quarter:

1. **Engineering Notebook.** Every entry gets a heading: your name and period, the date, and a title. Engineers keep notebooks so that anyone — including future you — can follow what was done and why.
2. **Build materials.** You request them, you use them, you return them to the same place. Shared tools get put back the way you found them.
3. **Tool safety.** Scissors, glue guns, and calipers get used the way they are demonstrated. Nothing else.
4. **CTLS.** Log in now and make sure it works. Daily instructions live there.

### About Me hexagons

You get a sheet of 14 hexagons, each with a category. Fill in **seven** of them — any seven. Mr. Willingham will model one (Place) so you can see how much to write.

Then, in pairs or small groups, share what you wrote. Listen for something you have in common with each person. Your photo gets pasted on the sheet before it is collected.

## Closing: Meet Your Team

Teams are already assigned. When yours is called, move to your team's table and introduce yourselves. You will be working with these people for the next few weeks.

One preview, and only one: **tomorrow you become engineers.** A real medical design problem, for a real patient in need. That is all you get today.

Two things to bring:

- **Your composition notebook,** if you do not have it yet.
