## Objectives

- I can finish and test an AFO prototype against the criteria and constraints in the design brief.
- I can document my team's process — materials, drawings, roles, and results — in the notebook.
- I can compare my team's prototype to real-world AFO designs.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Where Did We Stop?

Sit with your team and read your pause-point note from yesterday. Then re-read the **design requirements** in the brief. That list is what you are testing against today — not how it looks, not how much glue it took.

Remember: this is a **prototype**, not a final product. It has to work well enough to test and to explain.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] My team knows exactly what is left to build.
- [ ] I re-read the design requirements.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Finish and Test

You have most of the period. Use it like this:

1. **Finish the build.** Whatever is left from yesterday.
2. **Test it.** The Tester puts it on. Does it support the ankle? Does the foot still move the way the movement information says it must? Does it stay on?
3. **Fix what you can.** One design, one prototype — but you can still adjust it.
4. **Finish the documentation.** The Recorder makes sure Entry #1 has materials, drawings, roles, and now **results and observations** — what happened when you tested it.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
There is a firm stop time. When it is called, hands off — a prototype that is 90% done and documented beats one that is 100% done and has nothing written down.
{{< /callout >}}

### The real thing

After the stop time, we look at the **sample AFO images** — the ones worn inside a shoe, the ones worn without, and the articulated (hinged) kind. Compare them to yours. What did the real designers do that you did? What did they do that you did not think of?

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Our prototype is finished and was tested on a real ankle.
- [ ] Entry #1 has materials, a drawing, roles, and results.
- [ ] I can name one thing a real AFO does that ours does or does not.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing: Share-Outs

Each team shares its prototype from its table — the **Presenter** talks, but anyone can answer questions. Keep it short: what you built, how it did in the test, and one thing you would change if you had a second try.

Tomorrow: the official design process, and how it compares to what you actually did this week.

{{% /closing %}}
