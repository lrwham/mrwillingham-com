{{< icon "calendar" >}} **Wednesday, August 5th, 2026**

{{% alert "Short period today" %}}
Today's class is 25 minutes because of a school admin session. That is about 20 minutes of build time. Move fast, and leave your prototype in a state you can pick back up tomorrow.
{{% /alert %}}

{{% objectives %}}

## Objectives

- I can start building an AFO prototype from recycled materials, using the time I have.
- I can leave my prototype and my notebook in a state my team can resume tomorrow.

{{% /objectives %}}

{{% warmup %}}

## Warmup: Materials and Plan

Recycled materials get sorted and handed out to teams. While that happens, pull out Entry #1 and look at your team's sketches from yesterday and the design brief. Decide as a team which idea you are building — there is only time for one.

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Warmup

- [ ] My team has its materials.
- [ ] My team picked one design to build.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /warmup %}}

{{% worksession %}}

## Work Session: Build

Build your AFO from the recycled materials plus the kit materials. Test as you go — the **Tester** tries it on a real ankle and reports back to the team.

The **Recorder** documents while the team builds, not after:

- Which materials you used, and for what.
- A drawing of what you are building.
- Who did what.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Scissors and glue the way they were demonstrated. Shared tools go back where they came from.
{{< /callout >}}

{{% checkpoint %}}

### Checkpoint: Work Session

- [ ] Our prototype has a start — something is built, not just planned.
- [ ] The Recorder has materials, a drawing, and roles in the notebook.

{{% /checkpoint %}}

{{% /worksession %}}

{{% closing %}}

## Closing: Pause Point

Two minutes before the end: write in the notebook exactly where your team stopped and what is left for tomorrow. Put your prototype in your team's storage spot — **do not take it apart.** Tomorrow you finish, test, and share.

{{% /closing %}}
