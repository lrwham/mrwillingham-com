## Warmup: A Hurt Ankle

Have you ever sprained or hurt your ankle? What could help hold it steady while it heals — and still let you walk?

Talk it through at your table for two minutes, then set up **Notebook Entry #1**:

- Today's date at the top.
- Title: **Activity 1.1: Foot Orthosis Instant Design Challenge**

Then we watch the Instant Design Challenge introduction video together.

## Work Session: The Design Brief

Move to your team's table. Add your team members, your group number, and everyone's role to Entry #1.

### Read the brief

Your team reviews the **Design Brief** — it is in Activity 1.1 on myPLTW and printed as a backup. It has five sections:

- **Client** — who you are designing for.
- **Problem statement** — what is wrong.
- **Design statement** — what you are being asked to make.
- **Design requirements** — what the solution must do. These are your criteria and constraints.
- **Movement information** — how the ankle has to be able to move.

Read all five. Then, as a class, we define **cerebral palsy** in your own words and talk through what the brief is actually asking for.

In Entry #1, write one short statement for each of the five sections — in your words, not copied.

### Key Vocabulary

Prototype
: A first working model of a solution, built to test an idea. Not a finished product.

Instant Design Challenge
: One design, one prototype, no time to rebuild. You plan first, because you only get one try.

Criteria
: The things a solution must do to be successful.

Constraints
: The limits you have to work inside — materials, time, size.

### Brainstorm and sketch

Now your team brainstorms. Lots of ideas, sketched fast in your notebooks. No idea gets shot down yet. The **Recorder** makes sure every sketch and every role ends up on paper.
