## Unit: Introduction to Design

Welcome to Design and Modeling. This week you get set up in the classroom and in your **Engineering Notebook**, and then you get dropped straight into a real medical design problem — an ankle-foot orthosis (AFO) for a child with cerebral palsy — before anyone teaches you a design process. Your team reads the design brief, brainstorms and sketches, builds a prototype from recycled materials you collected yourselves, and tests it against the brief. On Friday you watch the design process videos and discover you were following the process all along. The week closes with team norms, a decision matrix to pick the class's strongest AFO, and the reveal of the quarter-long Unit Problem: a therapeutic toy for a child with cerebral palsy.

## Week 2 Preview

Next week you learn to communicate a design the way engineers do: with sketches. You will make thumbnail, isometric, and multiview sketches of real objects, then switch to measurement — reading rulers to 1/16 inch and 0.1 cm — and build a paper skimmer from a dimensioned sketch.
