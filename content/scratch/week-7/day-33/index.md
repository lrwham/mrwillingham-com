## Objectives

- I can design a Pokémon (name, type, six stats, height, weight) and explain my choices.
- I can use box plots, scatter plots, and histograms to compare my design against real Pokémon.
- I can revise stats that look wrong based on what the plots show.
- I can save my Pokémon as a `.csv` and sketch it on paper.

{{% /objectives %}}

## Work Session: Design, Test, Revise

Follow **Design, Test, Revise** on the project page. Decide the personality, type, and battle role before you type a number. Then try at least one box plot, one scatter plot, and one histogram, fix any stat that looks wrong, and plot again. Let the data argue with you.

Finish with **5. Save and quit** and a filename like `your_pokemon_name.csv`.
