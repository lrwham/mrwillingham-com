## Unit: Conditionals and Control Flow

This week you make your maze interactive with keyboard controls and wall detection using conditionals. You practice conditional logic through vocabulary, offline flow diagrams, and a look at loops, then finish the week by combining loops and conditionals to build a working game mechanic from a blank project.
