{{< icon "calendar" >}} **Thursday, December 17th, 2026** — *Early Release*

A short period, so two quick activities: a word search with a partner, then a crossword on your own. Both pull from every vocabulary word of the quarter — Scratch blocks, conditionals, the platformer, Python, and AI. The lists are on the [Unit 1](/scratch/reference/unit-1-vocab/), [Unit 2](/scratch/reference/unit-2-vocab/), and [Unit 3](/scratch/reference/unit-3-vocab/) vocabulary pages.

## Activity 1: Word Search (Partners)

{{< button text="Download the Word Search (PDF)" >}}/downloads/end-of-year-word-search.pdf{{< /button >}}

1. Pair up with **one** partner and pick up one printed worksheet.
2. Find every word in the **Word Bank**. Words run forward, backward, up, down, and diagonally.
3. When you have them all, raise your hand. The first groups to finish get a treat.

## Activity 2: Crossword (Solo)

{{< button text="Download the Crossword (PDF)" >}}/downloads/end-of-year-crossword.pdf{{< /button >}}

1. Pick up your **own** worksheet — no partners.
2. Fill in every square from the **Across** and **Down** clues.
3. When every square is correct, raise your hand. Only the first three finishers get a treat.
