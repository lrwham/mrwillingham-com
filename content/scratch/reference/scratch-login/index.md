## Your Class Account

Mr. Willingham created a Scratch {{< icon "scratch" >}} account for you to use in this class. The username and password are deliberately random and not personally identifiable, to protect your privacy.

Do not use a personal Scratch account for class work. Projects must be in your class account so they can be graded.

## Finding Your Login

Your username and password were sent to your school email.

1. Open [Office 365](https://office365.cobbk12.org/) and sign in with your school email and password.
2. Open **Outlook** and look for an email from Mr. Willingham.
3. If you do not see it, check **Junk** and **Deleted Items**.
4. Still nothing? Raise your hand.

## Logging In

1. Go to [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu).
2. Click **Sign in** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter the username and password from the email.
4. Click **Create** to start a new project, or **My Stuff** (under your username) to open an old one.

## Saving

Scratch saves automatically most of the time, but not always. Press `cmd + S` or use **File → Save now** before you close the tab or switch projects.

## If Scratch Logs You Out

Scratch sometimes signs you out mid-class, and unsaved work is lost.

- Sign back in and check **My Stuff** — the project may be there with most of your work.
- If it is gone, use the **starter project** link on the lesson page. Click **Remix** to get your own copy and pick up where the lesson is.
- Save more often from now on.

## Sharing Projects

Projects are private until you share them. When a lesson says to share, see [Share to the Class Studio](/scratch/reference/share-to-studio/).
