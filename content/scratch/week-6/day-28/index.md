## Work Session: Rehearse, Swap, Revise

Do these **in order**.

### 1. Rehearse

Set a timer for five minutes and run the whole presentation out loud, on your feet, including the live Scratch demo. Under three minutes: fill in thin slides. Over five: cut. Fix anything obvious before you swap.

### 2. Present to Another Team

Present to your assigned partner team, then watch theirs. Play their prototype like a player who has never seen it — do not ask them questions. Fill out the [Peer Feedback Worksheet](/scratch/projects/video-game-design/peer-feedback-worksheet/) for them:

1. **What works well?** One specific thing.
2. **What is confusing or broken?**
3. **One suggestion** — concrete and actionable.
4. **Presentation feedback** — what was clear, what could be clearer.

Honest and kind. "It's bad" and "it's good" are both useless; say what specifically and what would fix it.

### 3. Revise

Read every comment you received. As a team, pick the **single most important fix** to the prototype and the single most important fix to the presentation. Bugs first, missing mechanics second, polish last. Playtest after every fix. Then do one more timed run-through.
