## Work Session: Split the Work

### Prototype (Scratch)

1. Create a new project named `[Studio Name] — [Game Title]` and share it so teammates can access it.
2. Build the **most important mechanic** first. Platformer: moving and jumping. Puzzle: one solvable level. Chase or collection: the main character and one object.
3. No polish yet. Reuse the [Scratch Code Patterns](/scratch/reference/code-patterns/) and your earlier projects.

### Presentation (PowerPoint)

1. Download the template and open it in **PowerPoint Online** (Office 365 → PowerPoint).
2. Share it with every teammate and Mr. Willingham.
3. Fill in **Slides 1–5** with real content. Photograph the box art for Slide 6.
