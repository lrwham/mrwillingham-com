## Work Session: Presentations

Mr. Willingham announces the order. When your team is called:

1. Open the presentation on the classroom display.
2. Present the slides — 3–5 minutes, every member speaks.
3. From Slide 7, open Scratch and demo the game live.
4. Take questions.

Speak to the audience, not the screen. If the demo hiccups, stay calm and keep narrating.

While other teams present, be the audience you want: quiet, attentive, and ready with a question.
