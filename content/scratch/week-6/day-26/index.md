## Work Session: Priorities

| Priority | If… | Work on… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First** | The core mechanic does not work yet | Scratch — get the player moving and one rule working |
| **Second** | The mechanic works but the game is not complete | Scratch — scoring, a win/loss condition, or a second level |
| **Third** | The prototype is in good shape | PowerPoint — finish Slides 1–6 and insert concept art |

Split up: one teammate on Scratch, one on PowerPoint, unless the prototype urgently needs everyone.

**Prototype checklist:** core mechanic works; a win or loss condition; at least one real sprite and backdrop; no broken scripts.

**Presentation checklist:** Slides 1–5 filled with real content; Slide 6 concept art; an image on every slide; shared with the team and Mr. Willingham.
