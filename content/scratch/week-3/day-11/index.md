## Work Session: Build With an Operator

The `and`, `or`, and `not` blocks are green, in the **Operators** category, and fit inside the diamond slot of an `if` block.

Open Scratch and build a small project that uses at least **one** boolean operator. Ideas:

- A sprite that moves only when two keys are pressed at the same time (`and`)
- A sprite that changes color when touching either of two sprites (`or`)
- A sprite that falls when it is `not` touching the ground

Want to see why operators matter? The **Why Operators Shrink Your Code** section of the reference page shows the same game logic drawn with and without them.
