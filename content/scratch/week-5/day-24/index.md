## Work Session: Finish Both

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Both are due at the end of class. Do not spend the whole period on one and ignore the other.
{{< /callout >}}

### Box Art

Check every element against the [Box Art Assignment](/scratch/projects/video-game-design/box-art/) and [Rubric](/scratch/projects/video-game-design/box-art/rubric/).

- **Front:** title, artwork, studio name or logo, age rating.
- **Back:** tagline, 2–4 sentence description, at least two "screenshot" drawings, a 3–5 item features list, studio name or logo, a fictional platform logo or system requirement.

A box that looks finished scores better than one that looks rushed.

### Game Design Document

Open the shared GDD in [Office 365](https://office365.cobbk12.org/) and fill in every section with complete sentences: cover page, team roles, overview, gameplay, characters, world and setting, sounds and music. Confirm it is shared with Mr. Willingham as *Can edit*. An empty section costs points.

{{% checkpoint %}}
