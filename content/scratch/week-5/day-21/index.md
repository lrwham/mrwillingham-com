## Warmup: The Project

For the next nine class days you work in a team of up to three to design and build an original video game in Scratch. It must be appropriate for players ages 11–14 and cannot borrow artwork or characters from existing games. The finished project includes:

- **Concept art** (box art, drawn on paper)
- **Game Design Document** (Word Online) — [What is a GDD?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_design_document)
- **Presentation** (PowerPoint)
- **Prototype** (Scratch)

Everyone contributes to every part, but each person leads one role:

| Role | Leads |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead Programmer** | Writing and debugging the Scratch code |
| **Lead Writer** | The Game Design Document and the story |
| **Lead Artist** | Concept art, sprites, and backgrounds |
| **Lead Presenter** | The PowerPoint and the class demo |

Three members: one person leads two roles. Two members: split all four. Solo: you lead all four.
