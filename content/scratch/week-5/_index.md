## Unit: Intermediate Scratch

Monday wraps up the falling-objects game: take the unit quiz, polish your game, and share it to the class studio.

## Unit: Video Game Design Project

Tuesday you begin the final Scratch project — designing and building a video game with a team. The project runs through next Friday, the last day before Thanksgiving break. This week you brainstorm the concept, write a Game Design Document, and draw box art.
