## Unit: Introduction to Scratch

This week you learn what programming is, get set up in the computer lab, and start building in Scratch. Monday is a digital learning day you complete from home. The rest of the week is shortened by conference-week early release, so each lesson is sized to fit. By Friday you will have explored sequencing, events, motion blocks, and the Scratch art tools, and started a maze game with a partner.

## Week 2 Preview

Next week you bring your maze to life with keyboard controls and use conditionals to detect wall collisions. You deepen your understanding of conditionals through a Flocabulary lesson and an offline flow-diagram activity, then learn how loops let programs repeat actions efficiently. By the end of the week you combine loops and conditionals to build a working game mechanic from a blank project.
